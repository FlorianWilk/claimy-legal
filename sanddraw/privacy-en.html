Privacy policy for SandDraw

Updated November 13, 2026 · App version 1.0.1

SandDraw runs entirely on your device. The app requires no user account or internet permission. It contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Touch input and device motion and tilt readings are processed only on your device to move the grains. These inputs are not transmitted to the developer or any other recipient. The current simulation is held in memory and is not stored on a server.

Your selected material, finger width, and motion control setting are saved locally in the private app settings. Android app backups and device transfers are disabled for SandDraw. You can delete these settings using Android's “Clear storage” option or by uninstalling the app.

SandDraw does not collect or transmit personal data to the developer. No data is sold or used for advertising. The app does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files belonging to other apps.

Purchases and app delivery are handled by Google Play. Google's privacy information applies to account and payment data processed by Google. SandDraw does not receive payment details.
