Privacy Policy for Shiny Fluids

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Package: com.shinyfluids.app · App version 1.1.0

Shiny Fluids is an interactive 2D/3D fluid art app, Live Wallpaper, and Android TV Ambient Screensaver. We respect your privacy: the app requires no user account and does not access your contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone.

1. Local On-Device Processing & Storage

  • All fluid rendering, multi-touch interactions, custom presets, HD screenshots, and 10-second MP4 video exports are processed 100% locally on your device.
  • App settings, custom presets, and wallpaper preferences are stored locally in private Android SharedPreferences. You can delete them at any time via Android’s “Clear storage” setting or by uninstalling the app.
  • Screenshots (Pictures/Shiny Fluids) and videos (Movies/Shiny Fluids) are written to your device gallery only when you explicitly initiate an export.

2. Advertising (Free Tier on Phones & Tablets Only)

In the free version on smartphones and tablets, Shiny Fluids uses Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC) to display menu-only banner ads and occasional interstitial ads between preset switches.

  • No ads are ever displayed on Android TV / Google TV, inside the Live Wallpaper, inside the Android TV Screensaver, or after purchasing the one-time Pro Unlock.
  • To serve, cap, and measure ads, Google AdMob may process device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, basic diagnostic/crash information, and ad interaction metrics in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy.
  • Users in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland are shown Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent dialog and can review or withdraw consent at any time inside Studio → Look → Ad Privacy Settings.

3. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Shiny Fluids offers a single optional one-time Pro Unlock (pro_unlock) processed exclusively by Google Play Billing. There are no recurring subscriptions. We never receive or store payment card or billing details.

4. Children’s Privacy

Shiny Fluids is intended for a general audience (13+) and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable age of digital consent.
